How it works

Five things worth knowing

  • 1Each game takes eight to ten minutes and works on a phone, a laptop or a shared screen.
  • 2Enter your work email at the start of a game so your score lands on the leaderboard. It is used for nothing else.
  • 3Every game scores out of 1,000, so all four weeks count equally. Your best run per week is the one that counts.
  • 4Replay as often as you like. Scenarios are shuffled, and the debrief at the end is where most of the learning is.
  • 5Finish all four and you have covered the year's awareness curriculum in about forty minutes.
